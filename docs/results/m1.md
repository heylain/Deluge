## Caveats

Known before any result:

- **Parameter matching.** Every arm is matched to A0's 110.02M parameters to
  within 0.07%: conv 110.09M, LRU 109.95M, transformer 110.07M. Both the conv
  and the transformer come out slightly larger, so a mismatch works against
  CfC.
- **The conv's width differs.** The conv arm runs at `d_inner` 872 against
  CfC's 1152, because that is where its parameters match.
- **`b_g`.** CfC and the LRU carry a gate bias, initialised at 1.278, that
  spec §3 does not list. Without it the gate cannot start near 1 (design D7).
- **The λ grid.** The init reads spec §3's "log-uniform" as a geometric grid
  of λ over heads (memories of 999 to 9 tokens), not a linear one (design D6).
- **The LRU arm measures the conv only.** Δ is always 1 at A1, so CfC against
  the LRU is what the short conv is worth. Δ is A3's question.
- **The transformer is the hybrid's attention, not a tuned transformer's.**
  GQA 12/4, four learned sinks, RoPE θ = 1M, and the same init and schedule
  as the hybrid.
- **A0's gap, if it was carried forward.** If A0 was stable but behind and
  its diagnosis found no bug, the gap and the diagnosis are recorded here
  (design 8.3).
